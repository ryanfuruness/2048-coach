# Sample outputs of the analysis engine (2026-10-07 08:20, best_v6c + installed models)

Generated by the coordinator at the end of the 24-hour run, to show what the engine says today.

- `positions.txt`: four single positions, text output of `build/analyze --preset best --personal 0.03 BOARD`
  (an ordinary 512 position; a 1024 position with a blocked chain; an expert's block-formation position judged by the
  exact block tables; an unusual formation where the odds carry the "less certain" caveat).
- `review_nov00.txt` (novice, largest tile up to 512), `review_int01.txt` (intermediate, 1024-2048),
  `review_str01.txt` (strong, 4096-8192), `review_exp01.txt` (expert, 16384+): full game reviews of 207-300-move
  stretches from the curated human data (`build/analyze --preset best --review results/review/stretches/<id>.txt`).
  Each move is analysed in turn; the summary is at the end of each file (skill, accuracy, chances kept, phases, key
  moments, warnings, hints, hard moments).

How to read them: docs/guide.md (what the numbers mean and what the research found), docs/api.md (every field).
Notes: the first answers of a fresh process are slower (exact tables read from disk); 32768-phase positions get no
odds (the engine has never built a 65536 in 3,898 test games) and their "expected future points" are the engine's,
which collapses in that phase.
